# Decision 0011 · Daily Paper production/shadow pilot

Status: approved by direct Human PI instruction, 2026-09-29.

## Decision

Use the existing `每日论文` conversation and its single active heartbeat as the first ongoing research production/shadow workload for Lab Research OS. Preserve its private Skill and established three-paper workflow. Add cost-aware routing, evidence boundaries, resume-first behavior, natural infrastructure observations and a daily Human PI report.

Only Literature Radar and reporting are daily. Evidence/novelty synthesis is proposed weekly or on a significant-paper event. Research, data, figure, manuscript and reproducibility readiness use event, milestone or state triggers. This decision does not authorize new automations for those workloads.

Routine mechanical work must not reach Astra. Delegated model class and effort must be explicit; parent high effort is never inherited by default. Sol is Scientific Director/QA. Astra is reserved for architecture or unresolved structural conflicts. Human PI retains direction, consequential resources, irreversible changes and scientific acceptance.

## Evidence and adoption boundary

The prompt and contracts are configured now. They become live-qualified only after a real scheduled run provides evidence. Existing reports and model interpretations do not become validated knowledge automatically. Historical Daily Paper repair work remains partial and resumable; it is not restarted or declared complete by this decision.
