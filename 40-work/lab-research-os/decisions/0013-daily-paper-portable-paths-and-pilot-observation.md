# Decision 0013 · Daily Paper portable paths and bounded pilot observation

Status: approved by direct Human PI instruction, 2026-09-29.

## Decision

New Daily Paper human deliverables use short semantic filenames. The complete English paper title belongs inside the note and master index, not in a supervisor-note filename. New generated filenames must be at most 140 characters and their complete absolute paths at most 240 characters. New issues validate this with the opt-in `--require-portable-paths` gate alongside `--require-visuals`.

The completed note-073 Event pilot is accepted as evidence for bounded resume, source matching, rendered visual QA, local failure recovery, Daily Brief production and zero-default-Astra routing. It does not live-qualify the next scheduled new-issue workflow or the future visual contract, because no complete post-contract issue has yet passed both gates.

## Repair boundary

Two over-budget supervisor filenames in issue 021 were shortened after a direct-open failure exposed the defect. Their document content and internal full titles were preserved, and both repaired final paths opened directly in Word and rendered cleanly. A read-only archive scan found 34 other historical supervisor files still above the new budget. They remain a separate Event-triggered migration backlog; this decision does not authorize an unreviewed bulk rename or retroactively invalidate historical notes.
