# Decision 0015 — Laboratory-wide bounded retries and representative verification

Date: 2026-09-29. Owner: Human PI. Implementer: Sol/Codex maintenance turn.

The Human PI requires cost-effectiveness to govern failures across the whole laboratory, not only Daily Paper. The default maximum is three total attempts for one logical target, outcome and failure class, including equivalent routes to the same underlying artifact or service. A retry requires a plausible changed outcome and a lightweight benefit/cost calculation. Reaching the cap normally causes substitution, qualified fallback or a preserved continuation point, not a higher-cost model.

A major scientific finding or content that is directly important and irreplaceable to the active Human PI research may receive one finite extension of at most three attempts, provided its value, irreplaceability, cost, changed method and extension budget are recorded. Further work becomes a Human PI decision; infinite retries remain prohibited.

This decision authorizes one current proportional verification event across already managed infrastructure. It includes deterministic Foundation/routing/registry/contracts checks, live Codex App coordination already exercised by the Daily Paper trial, one bounded DSH headless check, automation inspection, and the real Daily Paper production run. It does not authorize synthetic scientific results, triggerless R/SPSS/data/figure/manuscript work, private cloud upload, blocked-plugin activation, credential changes or a second recurring automation.

The same authorization adopts passive cost/value telemetry. Each executed workload records a compact run-level summary in its existing log, using provider-reported tokens when available, a labelled estimate when defensible, or `unavailable` otherwise. The Human PI Daily Research Brief exposes only a compressed token range, model/effort mix, retry/stop events, high-reasoning anomalies and `B/C/EVR`. Measurement must not create an additional model workload or reveal private content.
