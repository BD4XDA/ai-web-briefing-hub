## Declared limitations and false-acceptance risks

1. The engineering pilots do not demonstrate marine-science validity, current harness availability, model authentication or general autonomous research. A new task's result must earn its own scoped acceptance.
2. E3/E5 show that worker/coordinator scripts can overstate meta-success. Model PASS, confidence alone, a checkpoint commit or a hash alone must not substitute for the actual acceptance contract.
3. E7/E8 distinguish test success, model review and adoption. F5/F6 remain **DISABLED_NOT_ACCEPTED**; successor **NOT READY**; C1 **NOT PROVEN**, C2/P5 **UNCERTAIN**, P6 **YELLOW**. This exit recommendation does not rescore or override any of them.
4. Independent review can only judge supplied evidence; omitted raw information or unsupported producer flags can mislead it. Future supervised packets must provide the relevant approved sources, and unknown provenance must not be promoted.
5. Themis/Argus meanings were not located; separate ongoing verification custody is plausible, and continuous code-QC ownership is a documented gap. Existing task-scoped owners can carry these functions; no current record proves a new permanent agent is necessary.
6. C-drive stale copy and historical absolute links can misdirect recovery. Use the registered D-root plus marker/checkpoint; no migration/cleanup or old-packet replay is authorized.
7. AT09 remains preliminary and unaccepted. Its capability claims were not used as accepted evidence here. Historical F4 unknowns remain explicitly unresolved.
